# SNS Sales Formula Analysis - 2026-07-27

## Mission constraint

Sell the US$9 AI First Sale Automation Template Pack ethically. No spam, no fake scarcity, no scraped outreach, no revenue guarantee.

## What successful sellers are doing

### 1. They do not sell from one post

Creator/product data points show that one-off posts are weak. NeoReach's 2026 creator report says most consumers need 2 to 3 exposures before buying, and only 12% buy after one exposure.

Application:
- Stop treating each post as a direct sale attempt.
- Build a repeat exposure path: pain post -> free workflow -> proof/market notes -> paid pack.

### 2. They build in public before asking for money

Gumroad's Chase Dimond launch breakdown emphasizes build-in-public and multi-channel distribution. Daniel Vassallo and Randall Kanna examples show the same pattern: publish useful expertise first, then package what people repeatedly ask for.

Application:
- Use the mission itself as public proof: zero revenue, market pivot, free workflow, test results, changes made.
- Make the buyer feel this pack was built from live battle conditions, not invented in a vacuum.

### 3. They use a bridge, not a cold checkout link

LinkedIn demand-building advice and modern social funnel guidance converge on the same structure: short-form discovery, lead magnet bridge, long-form trust builder, then conversion.

Application:
- Free n8n lead scoring workflow is the bridge.
- DEV tutorial and GitHub pages are the trust builders.
- Gumroad is the conversion step.

### 4. Trust beats price

ScrollSignal's 2026 social commerce research says shoppers mention trust three times more often than lower price. Clutch reports that 96% of consumers check reviews before first-time purchases. For a new product with no reviews, the substitute must be visible proof: live build notes, files, screenshots, clear refund-safe pricing, and honest limitations.

Application:
- Emphasize "downloadable files exist" and "no revenue guarantee" because fake certainty kills trust.
- Show exact contents, exact workflow names, and direct free JSON.
- Ask for first buyers to reply with what they used, then turn that into proof.

### 5. Pain-aware buyers need PAS; cold buyers need AIDA/BAB

PAS works when the buyer already feels pain: "tool cost keeps charging, revenue is 0." AIDA works when the buyer has not yet admitted the problem. BAB works for social posts because it shows a before/after bridge quickly.

Application:
- Primary social formula: PAS for direct pain.
- Secondary formula: BAB for transformation posts.
- Tertiary formula: AIDA for tutorial/SEO posts.

## Applied funnel

1. Attention: "Your AI automation is running. Today's deposit is still 0."
2. Interest: "The leak is usually before the workflow: wrong lead, weak pain, no paid next step."
3. Bridge: Free n8n AI Lead Capture Workflow.
4. Desire: Full pack adds paid-offer reply, follow-up, objection router, daily signal digest, scripts, landing template.
5. Action: US$9 Gumroad checkout.

## Channel policy

- Threads: do not use. Claude Code is using it.
- DEV: use for long-form/tutorial trust.
- GitHub Issues/Gist/Release: use as owned public proof surfaces.
- n8n Creator Hub: requires Creator login; prepared submission package.
- n8n Community Tutorials: requires login; prepared a free workflow tutorial post that leads with value, not a paid pitch.
- X/LinkedIn/Reddit/OKKY: use prepared posts only where account/community fit is confirmed. Avoid drive-by spam.

## Decision rule for next actions

- If a channel allows value-first tutorial content, publish.
- If a channel is expert-heavy and the product is beginner-oriented, do not pitch directly. Post the free workflow or market analysis only.
- If a channel requires login or community approval, prepare the exact post and queue it rather than pretending it is done.
- If a post gets no visible signal within a few hours, change the hook, not the product first.
