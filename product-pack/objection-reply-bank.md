# Objection Reply Bank

## "I do not have time"

That is why the pack is only five workflows. Do not install all of them at once. Start with lead capture and one follow-up. If that does not create a signal, stop.

## "I am not technical"

Use the Make/Zapier guide first. The n8n files are included for people who already use n8n, but the sales path is tool-independent.

## "Will this make money?"

No guaranteed product can honestly promise that. This helps you test whether your offer can create payment intent before buying another tool or course.

## "Why not buy a bigger workflow pack?"

Big packs are useful when you know exactly what to automate. This is for the earlier stage: turning attention or inquiries into a first paid offer path.

## "What if nobody buys?"

Then you learned before spending more. Keep the channel data, kill the weak offer, and test a narrower pain point.
