Most failed digital product launches are not product failures.

They are path failures.

The seller makes a Gumroad page, posts the link once, then waits.

That is not a funnel. That is a wish.

I looked at how creators and social sellers actually move buyers:

- short-form content creates discovery
- a free resource captures intent
- long-form or public proof builds trust
- a low-friction checkout converts the people already in pain
