# Market Research: AI Automation Template Products

Date: 2026-07-27 KST

## What buyers are already paying for

The strongest visible pattern is not "AI revenue diagnosis". Buyers are paying for concrete reusable assets:

- ready-to-import n8n JSON workflows
- Make/Zapier implementation guides
- lead capture, follow-up, CRM, proposal, customer support, and sales workflows
- large template libraries with clear file counts
- setup guides, screenshots, and commercial-use positioning

## Competitor pain points

The market is crowded with huge libraries, but their weak points are visible:

- too many files, not enough "what do I do first?"
- generic categories instead of a first-sale path
- buyers may not know which workflow to import first
- many products sell automation as productivity, not revenue validation
- setup anxiety: n8n/API keys/tools can still feel intimidating
- trust gap: large template counts can feel recycled or uncurated

## Our previous mistake

The prior offer sold diagnosis and offer thinking. That is useful, but it does not look as tangible as competing template packs.

For a tired beginner paying AI tool bills, a $9 product must immediately answer:

- What files do I get?
- What can I run today?
- How does this help me get a first sale?
- How is this simpler than buying a giant n8n dump?

## New product direction

Position away from "AI automation kit" and toward:

**AI First Sale Automation Template Pack**

Promise:

> A small, curated automation pack for turning one visitor, lead, or DM into a paid offer test within 48 hours.

Included:

- 5 n8n-style JSON workflow blueprints
- Make/Zapier implementation guide
- first paid offer landing template
- DM outreach and follow-up scripts
- email follow-up sequence
- objection reply bank
- 48-hour first-sale checklist

Differentiator:

Competitors sell hundreds or thousands of workflows. This sells the shortest path to a first payment.
